What Do We Cover in Discord Workshops?

Our online workshops are divided into specialized streams designed to help secondary, high school, and early college students excel.

Leadership & Public Speaking

Transform from a quiet classroom listener to a confident communicator. Learn debate structure, impromptu speaking, community organizing, and school club governance.

  • Speech structuring & stage fear overcoming
  • Scout leadership principles & club setup
  • Collaborative group simulation challenges
Target: All Secondary & +2

STEM Olympiad & Entrance Tactics

Master tricky problem-solving techniques for national Olympiads (Physics, Math, Astronomy) and prepare for competitive entrance tests (IOE, CEE, St. Xavier's, SOS, KMC).

  • Physics numerical breakdown strategies
  • Math shortcuts & logical reasoning tests
  • Exam time management & mental clarity
Target: Science Aspirants

Eco-Youth: Climate & Waste Action

Organized in partnership with youth environmental networks. Learn how to launch community tree plantation drives, school composting, and participate in global environmental initiatives.

  • Youth climate advocacy & grant ideation
  • School Eco-Club management templates
  • Project presentation & UN SDG alignment
Target: All Youth Leaders

Global Scholarships & Olympiad Roadmaps

Discover full scholarships for international undergraduate programs (US, Japan, India, Europe), building impressive extracurricular portfolios, and writing high-impact motivation essays.

  • Profile building from Grade 10 to 12
  • Standardized tests overview (SAT, IELTS, JLPT)
  • Guest talks from Nepali Ivy League scholars
Target: +2 & College Aspirants

Discord Workshop Community Guidelines

Navigo Nepal is committed to maintaining an encouraging, safe, and academically focused community for all Nepali youth.

Respect & Kindness

Treat every peer, mentor, and volunteer with mutual respect. Harassment, discrimination, foul language, or bullying results in an immediate permanent ban.

Stage Audio Etiquette

Keep your microphone muted during stage speaker presentations. When you wish to ask a question verbally, use the Discord "Raise Hand" button to be invited onto the speaker stage.

Zero Spam & Unauthorized Promotions

Do not send unsolicited private direct messages, promotional referral links, or unverified advertisements. Share only constructive educational resources.

Use Appropriate Channels

Post technical coding queries in #tech-and-code-labs, study doubts in #study-hall, and workshop feedback in #workshop-feedback to keep conversations organized.
