For Organizations

Become a Partner

Navigo Nepal collaborates with organizations, schools, and institutions committed to educational empowerment. Whether you're a school looking for student development programs, an NGO seeking youth engagement, or a corporate sponsor supporting education — we'd love to work with you.

Partnership Opportunities

Our partnerships have included organizations like UNICEF Nepal, USAID Clean Air, AIESEC Nepal, and Gurubaa Educational Content. Together, we've reached thousands of students across Nepal with impactful programs in career guidance, leadership development, and environmental stewardship.

Why Partner With Us?

We bring a dedicated team of young leaders, proven program methodologies, and a network of schools across all 7 provinces. Your partnership will directly impact students who need guidance the most — helping them navigate their futures with confidence and clarity.
