WHO WE ARE

About Navigo Nepal

Navigo Nepal is a youth-led, non-profit organization dedicated to empowering junior high school students to confidently navigate their futures by bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world skills. We aim to equip students with the essential knowledge, skills, and opportunities needed to become capable learners, responsible citizens, and global leaders.

Through interactive sessions, hands-on experiences, and nationwide outreach, we provide exposure to scholarships, career paths, and community leadership, encouraging students to explore their potential beyond the classroom. Our programs, which include informative seminars, competitions, and club management workshops, emphasize teamwork, personal development, and innovative thinking.

At Navigo Nepal, we are committed to transforming the educational landscape by fostering an environment where students can discover their passions, set meaningful goals, and contribute positively to their communities, guided by our mantra: Learn, Lead, and Lift.

THE CHALLENGE

The Problem We Solve

In Nepal, many students are confined to a narrow educational experience focused solely on academic achievements.

Lack of Exposure

Students lack exposure to different opportunities and have insufficient guidance on post-secondary options, creating a significant gap between what they learn and what they need to succeed.

Academic-Only Focus

The narrow focus on exams leaves students unprepared for practical challenges they will face in their careers and personal lives.

No Career Guidance

Students complete secondary education without understanding their next steps, leading to poor decision-making regarding further education and career choices.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower students across Nepal by transforming their educational experience from a narrow focus on academics to a more holistic approach that includes personal development, leadership, and problem-solving skills. We provide the exposure, guidance, and skills students need to navigate their educational and career paths successfully.

Our Vision

A future where every student in Nepal has the knowledge and tools to make informed decisions about their education and career. We envision a generation of confident, capable leaders who can contribute positively to their communities and the world.

What Makes Us Unique?

Navigo Nepal stands out because we don't just advocate for change — we actively create it. Our sessions are interactive and engaging, using games, discussions, and real-world scenarios to make learning fun and impactful. We focus on practical experiences, such as camps and workshops, to ensure students gain hands-on skills.

Our Approach

  • • Interactive learning through games and debates
  • • Hands-on practical experiences
  • • Tailored content for each school
  • • Pre and post-assessments
  • • Nationwide outreach programs

OUR TEAM

The Cohort Behind Navigo

Navigo Nepal was co-founded by Anupam Nyaupane, Biyog Dangol, and Prajwal Dhungana. Our first project included two additional members who have now become Alumni: Swapnil Sapkota, currently studying at New York University at Abu Dhabi, and Atal Raj Paudel, an incoming freshman at University of Texas at Arlington.

The Navigo Nepal team slowly grew along with its projects. Today, Navigo Nepal has 13 active members along with three Board of Advisors who work together to run Navigo Nepal as an organization.

Collaboration with Gurubaa

Gurubaa is Nepal's largest Educational Content Creator with over 780k followers across YouTube and over 128 million views. Navigo Nepal has partnered with Gurubaa with a goal of providing exposure to students for their bright future ahead.

OUR JOURNEY

Timeline of Impact

From our first session to nationwide expansion — key milestones in the Navigo Nepal story.

January 2024

Project planning begins. First preliminary survey conducted on 100+ students across different districts to understand their learning challenges.

April 2024

What's Next? campaign launches in Nuwakot and Dhading with first personality development sessions.

June 2024

USAID Clean Air and UNICEF collaboration for World Environment Day. Official Partnership with Gurubaa announced.

July 2024

Partnership with AIESEC for World's Largest Lesson. Nationwide expansion across Banke and Kathmandu.
