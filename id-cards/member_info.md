## 🚀 Easy Printing for Upcoming Members

For any upcoming new member added via the **ID Card Generator Portal** or appended to `members.json`:
- Their card URL is automatically generated as `https://navigonepal.org/idcard/{N}` (where `{N}` is 18, 19, 20, etc.).
- The embedded QR code on both the Front and Back card faces automatically encodes this clean URL.
- When scanned, it immediately loads their verified identity profile.
