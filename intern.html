About the Internship

Navigo Nepal's internship program is designed for individuals who want to go beyond volunteering and take on more structured, responsibility-driven roles within the organization. As an intern, you will be embedded in one of our core teams — programs, content development, outreach, or operations — and will work on real projects that directly impact students and schools across Nepal. Unlike traditional internships, ours is hands-on from day one. You will not be fetching coffee or filing paperwork. You will be designing sessions, coordinating with schools, analyzing data, and contributing to the strategic direction of Navigo Nepal's programs.

Our internship periods typically run for 2–3 months, with flexible start dates depending on the availability of projects. We accept interns throughout the year and tailor the experience to match your skills, interests, and career goals. Whether you are a student looking for practical experience, a recent graduate exploring career options, or a professional seeking a meaningful sabbatical, our internship program offers something valuable.

Internship Details

  • Duration: 2–3 months
  • Type: Part-time or full-time
  • Location: All Over Nepal (Hybrid / Remote / Field)
  • Stipend: Performance-based opportunities
  • Certificate upon completion
Specializations

Internship Tracks

Choose a track that aligns with your skills and career aspirations. Each track offers unique learning opportunities.

Program Design

Research, design, and refine session content. Work on presentation materials, activity design, and assessment tools for our programs.

School Outreach

Coordinate with schools, manage relationships, and handle logistics for program delivery. Be the bridge between Navigo Nepal and schools.

Communications & Media

Manage social media, create content, document events, and build Navigo Nepal's public presence and brand identity.

Learning Outcomes

What You Will Learn

An internship at Navigo Nepal is as much about your growth as it is about the impact we create together. You will develop practical skills that are directly applicable to careers in education, nonprofit management, social enterprise, and community development. Our team will mentor you, provide regular feedback, and help you build a portfolio of work that demonstrates your capabilities.

You will learn how to design and deliver educational programs, coordinate with schools and organizations, analyze impact data, and manage projects from conception to completion. You will develop communication and presentation skills through direct engagement with students and teachers. You will gain experience in nonprofit operations, including volunteer management, event planning, and strategic thinking. Most importantly, you will develop a deep understanding of the challenges and opportunities in Nepal's education landscape — knowledge that will serve you regardless of what your future career path looks like.

Mentorship

Every intern is paired with a coordinator or founding team member who serves as their mentor throughout the internship. Your mentor will guide you through your projects, provide career advice, and help you navigate the professional world. We believe mentorship is one of the most valuable aspects of any internship, and we are committed to making it a core part of your experience at Navigo Nepal.

Skills You Will Gain

  • Program design and facilitation
  • School and stakeholder coordination
  • Data collection and impact analysis
  • Content creation and presentation
  • Nonprofit management and operations
  • Leadership and team collaboration
Apply Now

How to Apply

To apply for an internship at Navigo Nepal, send your resume and a cover letter to navigonepal@gmail.com with the subject line "Internship Application: [Your Name] – [Track]". In your cover letter, tell us about your background, why you are interested in interning with Navigo Nepal, which track appeals to you, and what you hope to gain from the experience.

Our team reviews applications on a rolling basis and will respond within one week. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interview, after which offers will be extended. We encourage applications from individuals of all backgrounds and disciplines — what matters most is your passion for education and willingness to learn. Join us and be part of a team that is redefining what education means for Nepal's youth.

Requirements

  • Minimum 18 years of age
  • Currently enrolled or recent graduate
  • Strong communication skills in English and/or Nepali
  • Available for at least 2 months
  • Open to applicants across All Over Nepal (Remote / Field)
